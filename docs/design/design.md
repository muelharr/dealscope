# DealScope
Tagline:
Know Before You Buy.
## Product
DealScope is a Shopping Intelligence Platform that helps users compare products across multiple marketplaces before purchasing.
DealScope is NOT a marketplace.
Users cannot purchase products directly.
Instead, users analyze products using AI-powered shopping intelligence and then visit the original marketplace.
