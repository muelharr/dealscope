Privacy Policy

At DealScope, your trust is our highest priority. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our shopping intelligence platform.

1. Introduction

DealScope ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of personal information that is collected through our website, mobile application, and related services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of personal information we collect include:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials when you register.
  • Shopping Preferences: Product watchlists, price alerts, and browsing history within the DealScope platform.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, operating system, and browser information.
  • Transaction History: We do not store credit card details; however, we may track purchase confirmation IDs from affiliate partners to verify rewards.

3. How We Use Information

We use the data we collect to provide, maintain, protect, and improve our services. Specifically:

  • To provide personalized product recommendations and price trend alerts.
  • To analyze market trends and provide high-fidelity intelligence data.
  • To communicate with you regarding account updates or marketing (with your consent).

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including end-to-end encryption for sensitive data transfers and regular security audits of our infrastructure.
