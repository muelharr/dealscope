Our proprietary high-frequency tracking engine monitors the top 500+ global retailers every 15 minutes. For high-demand items or volatile flash sales, our "Turbo" algorithm increases frequency to every 60 seconds to ensure you never miss a price drop or an out-of-stock event.

DealScore is a weighted metric (0-100) calculated by analyzing historical price trends, current market demand, stock levels across competitors, and merchant reliability. A score above 85 typically indicates an "Optimal Buy" signal, representing the lowest recorded price in the last 180 days.

Privacy is our baseline. DealScope utilizes AES-256 bank-level encryption for all user data. We never sell your browsing history or purchase habits to third-party retailers. Our business model is supported by premium subscriptions, not your personal data profile.

We currently track over 1,200 retailers across North America and Europe, including major marketplaces like Amazon, Walmart, and Target, as well as specialized niche electronics and fashion boutiques. You can view the full list in our Integrations portal.
